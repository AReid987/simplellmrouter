# Product Guidelines: SimpleLLMRouter

## Tone and Voice
All external communications for SimpleLLMRouter, including documentation, marketing materials, and user interfaces, will adopt a blend of **Technical and Informative** and **Professional and Empowering** tones. This means:
*   **Technical and Informative:** Content will be precise, accurate, and provide detailed explanations to cater to a developer audience. Jargon will be used appropriately where it adds clarity and technical accuracy, but will be explained where necessary.
*   **Professional and Empowering:** Messaging will emphasize the reliability and efficiency of the SimpleLLMRouter. It will highlight how the tool empowers users to optimize their LLM usage, preserve quotas, and streamline their development workflows. The language will be confident, authoritative, and focused on benefits.

## Visual Identity and Branding
The visual identity and branding for SimpleLLMRouter will convey a blend of **Modern and Minimalist** and **Data-Driven and Analytical** aesthetics. This approach aims to communicate:
*   **Modern and Minimalist:** The design will feature clean lines, simple color palettes, and an uncluttered layout. This will convey efficiency, ease of use, and a forward-thinking approach.
*   **Data-Driven and Analytical:** Visuals will incorporate elements such as subtle charts, graphs, and structured layouts to emphasize the intelligent routing, quota tracking, and analytical capabilities of the product. This will reinforce the precision and data-informed decision-making inherent in the router's operation.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
The User Experience (UX) of SimpleLLMRouter's interfaces (e.g., CLI, potential future web UI) will be guided by the principles of **Efficiency and Speed**, **Clarity and Transparency**, and **Developer-Centric Design**.
*   **Efficiency and Speed:** Interfaces will be designed to prioritize quick access to information and minimize the number of steps required to perform tasks. This includes streamlined command-line interactions and intuitive navigation for any graphical interfaces.
*   **Clarity and Transparency:** Users will be provided with a clear understanding of the system's operations, especially regarding LLM routing decisions, quota usage, and fallback mechanisms. Information will be presented clearly and concisely to avoid ambiguity.
*   **Developer-Centric Design:** The design will align with common patterns and conventions familiar to developers. This means utilizing command-line interfaces effectively, providing clear API documentation, and ensuring that any graphical elements enhance, rather than hinder, a developer's workflow.

## Error Handling and Alerts
Error messages and alerts within the SimpleLLMRouter will adhere to the principles of being **Clear and Actionable**, **Contextual and Timely**, and utilizing **Severity-Based Visual Cues**.
*   **Clear and Actionable:** Error messages will provide concise explanations of the problem, suggest concrete next steps for resolution, and include references or links to relevant documentation where users can find more detailed information.
*   **Contextual and Timely:** Alerts and error messages will be displayed directly within the relevant interface (e.g., CLI output, logs), appearing only when necessary to avoid overwhelming the user with unnecessary notifications.
*   **Severity-Based Visual Cues:** Distinct visual indicators (e.g., color-coding for different log levels like INFO, WARN, ERROR, CRITICAL; specific icons) will be used to quickly communicate the severity and urgency of an issue, allowing users to prioritize their attention.
